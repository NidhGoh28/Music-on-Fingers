wave sculptor

A glowing string stretches between your hands and runs through all ten fingertips. Its shape is the sound wave, so your fingers sculpt the tone you hear.

〰️Soft, curved fingers make a smooth, pure tone. Spiky, spread fingers make it bright and buzzy. ↔️Hands apart is a longer string, so a lower note. Closer is higher. Notes snap to the raag. ↕️Raise your hands to play louder. Make a fist to go silent. 🔁Loop records 8 seconds, then plays it back so you can layer up to three voices.
Hand mode needs good light and both hands in frame.
